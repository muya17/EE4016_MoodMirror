===== COMMENTED BLOCKS IN LATEX =====

All presentation slide images have LaTeX \begin{figure}[h!] ... \end{figure} blocks
already commented out in FINAL_TECHNICAL_REPORT.tex. To activate them:

1. Search for: "% \begin{figure}[h!]"
2. Remove the "% " prefix from lines until you reach "% \end{figure}"
3. Ensure ppt_as_img/XX.jpg paths are correct
4. Recompile: pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error FINAL_TECHNICAL_REPORT.tex

Example (before):
  % \begin{figure}[h!]
  %     \centering
  %     \includegraphics[width=0.9\textwidth]{ppt_as_img/11.jpg}
  %     \caption{SimpleCNN architecture...}
  % \end{figure}
