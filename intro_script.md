# Intro Script (Under 2 Minutes)

Hi everyone, I am Zachariah Muya, and on behalf of Group 10, welcome to our project: MoodMirror.

Before we begin, let me quickly show a few facial expressions. We all do this naturally every day, because facial cues are one of the main ways humans communicate emotion and intent.

That is why facial expression recognition is such an active research area. It has practical value in many domains: human safety, road and traffic monitoring, assistive systems, and wellbeing support. In digital systems, if users show persistent signs of distress while repeatedly asking harmful or high-risk questions, emotion-aware guardrails could help escalate support or trigger safer interventions.

Our core problem is this: very large models can be accurate, but they are expensive to train and deploy. On the other hand, very small edge models are efficient, but often lose too much accuracy.

So our question was: can we find a practical middle ground for daily use?

In this project, we built and compared classical and deep-learning FER pipelines, then integrated them into a live web app demo with smart sampling for efficient inference.

In the next few minutes, we will walk you through our methodology, key results, and live demo step by step.
